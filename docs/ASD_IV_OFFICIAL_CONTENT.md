## Official rules (pp.39–40)

- **8 minutes for a standard speech; 4 minutes for reply speech.** (p.39)
- First and last minute of a standard speech are protected; no points of information (POIs) then. (p.39)
- No POIs during reply speech. (p.39)
- The 45 seconds following a POI, accepted or refused, are protected. (p.39)
- Sides are assigned by random draw. (p.40)
- Debate should remain respectful and never become personal. (p.40)

**Important timer distinction:** The product request specifies a **red visual warning after 5 minutes**, which is **not** the official time limit.
